# Prepared PCA phases

1. Ground: complete. Verify current source and trace data, numerical contracts, and test entry points.
2. Sketch: complete. Compare two bounded designs, cross-judge, pick, graft, verify.
3. Agree: complete. Proceed without a checkpoint under the user's instruction to get started.
4. Implement: complete. Simplify unused paths, add prepared fitting/projection, preserve existing caller behavior.
5. Scrap: complete. The selected design held through implementation and validation; no replacement was needed.

Engineering records stay outside the production branch.
